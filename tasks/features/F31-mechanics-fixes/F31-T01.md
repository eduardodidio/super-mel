## User Story

As a player, I want flying to last a maximum of 5 seconds so that there is a gameplay challenge instead of unlimited flight.

## Objective

Adicionar timer de voo de 5 segundos. Mel NAO comeca voando. Voo ativa ao segurar Space no ar apos 0.25s de hold. Apos 5s voando, voo desliga e Mel cai. Timer reseta ao tocar o chao.
