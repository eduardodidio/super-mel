# Task F26-T01 — Design Test Level Layout Data

**Feature:** F26
**Wave:** 0
**Type:** frontend
**Depends on:** (none)
**Status:** planned

## User Story

As a developer, I want a pre-defined test level layout, so that I can test all gameplay mechanics in controlled conditions.

## Objective

Create a test level data file that defines block positions, platforms, gaps, coins, hearts, and destructibles in a deterministic layout. The level should exercise all core mechanics: walking, jumping, crouching, flying, attacking destructible blocks, collecting items.

## Dev Notes

### File: `packages/frontend/src/game/systems/TestLevelData.ts` (NEW)

Export a function `generateTestLevel(): Chunk[]` that returns 4-5 pre-defined chunks forming a complete test level.

**Level design (~80 blocks wide = 5 chunks of 16):**

Chunk 0 (x: 0-15) — "Spawn & Basics":
- Flat ground at y=0 (dirt surface, stone below) from x=0 to x=15
- 3 coins at y=2 (x=3, x=5, x=7) — basic collection test
- 1 heart at y=2, x=12
- Sign area: stack of 2 wood blocks at x=1 (visual landmark)

Chunk 1 (x: 16-31) — "Platforms & Gaps":
- Ground with gap from x=20 to x=23 (4-block gap — requires jump)
- Platform at y=3 from x=17 to x=20 (brick, 4 wide) — test jump-to-platform
- Higher platform at y=6 from x=24 to x=27 (stone, 4 wide) — test fly
- 5 coins arc over the gap (y=3 to y=5)
- 1 heart on high platform (x=25, y=7)

Chunk 2 (x: 32-47) — "Destructibles & Combat":
- Ground continues flat
- Wall of wood blocks at x=35 (3 high) — test Bola do Infinito
- Wall of glass blocks at x=40 (2 high) — another destructible test
- Iron wall at x=44 (3 high) — indestructible, must jump over
- Coins behind wood wall (x=36-37, y=1-2)
- 1 item_block at x=38, y=4

Chunk 3 (x: 48-63) — "Advanced Platforming":
- Ground with 2 gaps: x=50-52, x=57-60
- Stepping stones: single blocks at y=2 (x=51), y=4 (x=53), y=3 (x=58)
- High platform at y=8 from x=54 to x=56 — fly test
- Coins at varying heights along the path
- Lava pit at bottom of second gap (x=57-60, y=-4)

Chunk 4 (x: 64-79) — "Victory Run":
- Flat ground, easy
- Cluster of 10 coins (celebration)
- 2 hearts
- Stack of item_blocks as "finish line" at x=75

**Data format**: reuse existing `Chunk`, `BlockData`, `HeartData`, `CoinData` interfaces from ChunkGenerator.ts.
